# Ubuyashiki Mansion

> [!locations] At a glance
> [[Demon Slayer Corps|The Corps]]' headquarters, home of [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki]].

## Overview

The Ubuyashiki Mansion is the headquarters of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] and the home of [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki]] and the [[Ubuyashiki Clan]]. It is where the [[Hashira]], including [[Tengen Uzui]] and [[Giyu Tomioka]], receive their orders.
