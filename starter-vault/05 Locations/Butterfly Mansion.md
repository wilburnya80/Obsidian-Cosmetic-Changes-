# Butterfly Mansion

> [!locations] At a glance
> [[Shinobu Kocho]]'s recovery estate for wounded slayers.

## Overview

The Butterfly Mansion is the estate of [[Shinobu Kocho]], where wounded members of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] recover after battles such as the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]]. [[Tanjiro Kamado]] and his friends are regular patients.

## Key facts

- **Owner:** [[Shinobu Kocho]]
- **Purpose:** recovery
- **Patients:** [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Inosuke Hashibira]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]]
