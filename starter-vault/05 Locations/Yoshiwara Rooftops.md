# Yoshiwara Rooftops

> [!locations] At a glance
> The tiled rooftops above the [[Entertainment District]], [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]]'s stage.

## Overview

The Yoshiwara Rooftops are the tiled roofs over the [[Entertainment District]]. This is where [[Tengen Uzui]] fights [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]], and the perch for the image this theme recreates: the [[Rooftop Fireworks Stand]], with the [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]] drawn against a sky of fireworks.
