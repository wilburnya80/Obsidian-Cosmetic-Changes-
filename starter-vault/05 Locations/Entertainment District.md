# Entertainment District

> [!locations] At a glance
> The lantern-lit pleasure quarter where the arc takes place.

## Overview

The Entertainment District, also called the Yoshiwara, is where the [[Entertainment District Arc]] unfolds. Its okiya [[Tokito-ya]], [[Ogimoto-ya]] and [[Kyogoku-ya]] hide the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]], and its tiled [[Yoshiwara Rooftops]] host the fight between [[Tengen Uzui]] and the demons [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]].
