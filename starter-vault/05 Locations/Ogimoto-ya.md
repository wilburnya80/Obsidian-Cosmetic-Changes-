# Ogimoto-ya

> [!locations] At a glance
> One of the three okiya in the [[Entertainment District]] used in the infiltration.

## Overview

Ogimoto-ya is one of the three okiya of the [[Entertainment District]] used during the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]]. [[Tengen Uzui]]'s wives [[Makio]], [[Suma]] and [[Hinatsuru]] and the disguised [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] and [[Inosuke Hashibira]] are spread across these houses to search for [[Daki]]. Together with [[Tokito-ya]], Ogimoto-ya and [[Kyogoku-ya]], the houses form the stage of the [[Entertainment District Arc]].
