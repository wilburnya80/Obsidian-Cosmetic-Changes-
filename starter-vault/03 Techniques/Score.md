# Score

> [!techniques] At a glance
> [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]]'s ability to read an opponent's rhythm and plan a fight like sheet music.

## Overview

The Score is how [[Tengen Uzui]] reads a fight: he listens for an opponent's rhythm, writes the whole exchange out in his head like a piece of music and then acts on it. It underpins [[Sound Breathing]] and forms such as [[String Performance]] and [[Constant Resounding Slashes]].
