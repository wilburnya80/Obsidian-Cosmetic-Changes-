# Hinokami Kagura

> [!techniques] At a glance
> [[Tanjiro Kamado]]'s inherited fire dance, used in his fights including against [[Gyutaro]].

## Overview

Hinokami Kagura, the Dance of the Fire God, is the technique passed down through the family of [[Tanjiro Kamado]]. He uses it with his [[Nichirin Blade]], and it is a core part of how he fights [[Gyutaro]] in the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]].
