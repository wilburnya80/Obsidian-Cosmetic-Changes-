# Sound Breathing

> [!techniques] At a glance
> [[Tengen Uzui]]'s [[Breathing Styles|Breathing Style]], built on rhythm, explosions and the [[Score]].

## Overview

Sound Breathing is the [[Breathing Styles|Breathing Style]] of [[Tengen Uzui]], the [[Tengen Uzui|Sound Hashira]]. It is performed with the [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]] and guided by his [[Score]], and the forms seen in the [[Entertainment District Arc]] include [[Constant Resounding Slashes]] and [[String Performance]]. It pairs naturally with [[Explosive Grenades]].
