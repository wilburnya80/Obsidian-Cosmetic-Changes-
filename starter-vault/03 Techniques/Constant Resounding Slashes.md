# Constant Resounding Slashes

> [!techniques] At a glance
> First Form of [[Sound Breathing]]: a rapid barrage of slashes from both cleavers.

## Overview

Constant Resounding Slashes is the First Form of [[Sound Breathing]], a rapid combination of cuts made with the [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]] by [[Tengen Uzui]]. It is the technique most associated with his opening attacks in the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]].
