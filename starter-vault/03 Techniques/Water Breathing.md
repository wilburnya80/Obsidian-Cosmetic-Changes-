# Water Breathing

> [!techniques] At a glance

## Overview

Water Breathing is the [[Breathing Styles|Breathing Style]] of [[Giyu Tomioka]], the [[Giyu Tomioka|Water Hashira]], and the style that [[Tanjiro Kamado]] first learned. It is one of the [[Breathing Styles]] and sits alongside [[Sound Breathing]] and [[Thunder Breathing]] in the [[Demon Slayer Corps]].
