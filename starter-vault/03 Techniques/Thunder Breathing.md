## Overview

Thunder Breathing is the [[Breathing Styles|Breathing Style]] of [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]], centred on lightning-fast strikes made with a [[Nichirin Blade]]. It is one of the many [[Breathing Styles]] in the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] and appears in the fight against [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]].
