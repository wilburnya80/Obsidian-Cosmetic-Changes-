# Shinobi Clan

> [!factions] At a glance
> The shinobi tradition [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]] and his wives came out of.

## Overview

The Shinobi Clan is the shinobi tradition that shaped [[Tengen Uzui]] and his wives [[Makio]], [[Suma]] and [[Hinatsuru]]. Their skills in stealth and disguise made the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]] possible, and [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]] carries that heritage into the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] as a [[Hashira]].

## Key facts

- **Alumni:** [[Tengen Uzui]], [[Makio]], [[Suma]], [[Hinatsuru]]
- **Skills:** stealth, [[Disguise and Infiltration]]
- **Now:** [[Uzui Household]]
