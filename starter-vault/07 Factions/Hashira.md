# Hashira

> [!factions] At a glance
> The nine elite swordsmen at the top of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]].

## Overview

The Hashira are the top-ranked members of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]]. [[Tengen Uzui]] is the [[Tengen Uzui|Sound Hashira]], [[Giyu Tomioka]] the [[Giyu Tomioka|Water Hashira]], [[Shinobu Kocho]] the [[Shinobu Kocho|Insect Hashira]] and [[Kyojuro Rengoku]] the [[Kyojuro Rengoku|Flame Hashira]]. They answer to [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki]] and face the [[Twelve Kizuki]].
