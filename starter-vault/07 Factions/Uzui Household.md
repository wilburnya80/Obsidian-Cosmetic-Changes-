## Overview

The Uzui Household is [[Tengen Uzui]] with his three wives [[Makio]], [[Suma]] and [[Hinatsuru]], who all come from the [[Shinobi Clan]] tradition. The household works as a team in the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]] and survives intact as [[Tengen Retires]].
