# Demon Slayer Corps

> [!factions] At a glance
> The secret organisation of swordsmen who hunt demons.

## Overview

The Demon Slayer Corps is the organisation led by [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki]] and the [[Ubuyashiki Clan]]. Its strongest members are the [[Hashira]], including [[Tengen Uzui]], [[Giyu Tomioka]], [[Shinobu Kocho]] and [[Kyojuro Rengoku]]. Members carry a [[Nichirin Blade]], learn [[Breathing Styles]] and operate from the [[Ubuyashiki Mansion]].

## Key facts

- **Leader:** [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki]]
- **Elite:** [[Hashira]]
- **Gear:** [[Nichirin Blade]]
- **Enemy:** [[Twelve Kizuki]], [[Muzan Kibitsuji]]
