# Ubuyashiki Clan

> [!factions] At a glance
> The clan that leads and funds the [[Demon Slayer Corps]].

## Overview

The Ubuyashiki Clan has led the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] for generations, currently through [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki]], and hosts the [[Hashira]] at the [[Ubuyashiki Mansion]]. Its long feud is with [[Muzan Kibitsuji]].
