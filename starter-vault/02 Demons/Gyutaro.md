# Gyutaro

> [!demons] At a glance
> [[Upper Rank Six]] demon, [[Daki]]'s brother, wielding poison blood and flying sickles.

## Overview

Gyutaro is [[Daki]]'s older brother and shares the [[Upper Rank Six]] position in the [[Twelve Kizuki]]. His [[Blood Demon Art]], the [[Flying Blood Sickles]], and his [[Poison]] make him deadly, and [[Gyutaro's Sickles]] are his signature weapons. [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Inosuke Hashibira]] and [[Tengen Uzui]] bring him down in the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]] in the [[Entertainment District]].
