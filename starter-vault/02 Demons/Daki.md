# Daki

> [!demons] At a glance
> [[Upper Rank Six]] demon who hides in the [[Entertainment District]] and attacks with living obi sashes.

## Overview

Daki is a demon of the [[Twelve Kizuki]] holding the rank of [[Upper Rank Six]] together with her brother [[Gyutaro]]. She hides in the [[Entertainment District]] among the courtesans, using [[Daki's Obi]] as weapon and trap. She is the first of the two siblings [[Tengen Uzui]], [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] and [[Inosuke Hashibira]] must bring down in the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]].

## Key facts

- **Rank:** [[Upper Rank Six]] (shared with [[Gyutaro]])
- **Weapon:** [[Daki's Obi]]
- **Hunting ground:** [[Entertainment District]]
- **Master:** [[Muzan Kibitsuji]]
