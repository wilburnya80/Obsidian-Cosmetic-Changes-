# Muzan Kibitsuji

> [!demons] At a glance
> Origin of all demons and master of the [[Twelve Kizuki]].

## Overview

Muzan Kibitsuji is the first demon and the source of every other demon in the story, including [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]]. He commands the [[Twelve Kizuki]] and is the long-term enemy of [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki]], the [[Ubuyashiki Clan]] and the entire [[Demon Slayer Corps]]. Like every demon he is bound by the [[Sunlight Weakness]].
