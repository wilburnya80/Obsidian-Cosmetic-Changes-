# Twelve Kizuki

> [!demons] At a glance
> [[Muzan Kibitsuji]]'s twelve strongest demons, ranked from Lower to Upper.

## Overview

The Twelve Kizuki are [[Muzan Kibitsuji]]'s elite demons, split into Upper Moons and Lower Moons. The [[Upper Rank Six]] seat is held jointly by [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]], the enemies of the [[Entertainment District Arc]]. The group is the primary target of the [[Hashira]] such as [[Tengen Uzui]].

## Key facts

- **Leader:** [[Muzan Kibitsuji]]
- **Notable seat:** [[Upper Rank Six]] ([[Daki]], [[Gyutaro]])
- **Opposed by:** [[Hashira]]
