# Tengen Retires

> [!events] At a glance
> [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]] steps down as [[Tengen Uzui|Sound Hashira]] after the arc.

## Overview

After the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]], [[Tengen Uzui]] steps down as [[Tengen Uzui|Sound Hashira]], ending his active service in the [[Demon Slayer Corps]]. His wives [[Makio]], [[Suma]] and [[Hinatsuru]] stay by his side as the [[Uzui Household]]. The retirement closes the [[Entertainment District Arc]].
