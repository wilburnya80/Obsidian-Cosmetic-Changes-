# Infiltration of Yoshiwara

> [!events] At a glance
> The undercover mission to find a demon inside the [[Entertainment District]].

## Overview

The Infiltration of Yoshiwara is the first phase of the [[Entertainment District Arc]]. [[Tengen Uzui]]'s wives [[Makio]], [[Suma]] and [[Hinatsuru]] enter the [[Entertainment District]] early; [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] and [[Inosuke Hashibira]] follow in disguise, placed in the okiya [[Tokito-ya]], [[Ogimoto-ya]] and [[Kyogoku-ya]]. The mission is a practical lesson in [[Disguise and Infiltration]].

## Key facts

- **Leader:** [[Tengen Uzui]]
- **Scouts:** [[Makio]], [[Suma]], [[Hinatsuru]]
- **Recruits:** [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]], [[Inosuke Hashibira]]
- **Targets:** [[Daki]]
