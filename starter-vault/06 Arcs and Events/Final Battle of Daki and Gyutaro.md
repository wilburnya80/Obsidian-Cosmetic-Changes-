# Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro

> [!events] At a glance
> The climactic fight of the arc against the [[Upper Rank Six]] siblings.

## Overview

The Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro is the climax of the [[Entertainment District Arc]]. [[Tengen Uzui]], [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] and [[Inosuke Hashibira]] fight [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]] across the [[Entertainment District]] and the [[Yoshiwara Rooftops]]. Key moments include [[Sound Breathing]]'s [[Constant Resounding Slashes]] and [[String Performance]], [[Hinokami Kagura]], the [[Flying Blood Sickles]] and [[Daki's Obi]]. It leads directly into [[Tengen Retires]].
