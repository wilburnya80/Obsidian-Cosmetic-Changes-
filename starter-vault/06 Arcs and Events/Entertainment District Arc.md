# Entertainment District Arc

> [!events] At a glance
> The arc where [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]] leads the infiltration of the [[Entertainment District|Yoshiwara]] and faces [[Upper Rank Six]].

## Overview

The Entertainment District Arc follows [[Tengen Uzui]] as he enlists [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] and [[Inosuke Hashibira]] to hunt a demon in the [[Entertainment District]]. It moves through the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]], the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]] and the aftermath where [[Tengen Retires]]. It follows the [[Mugen Train Arc]] and is covered in [[Anime Season 2]].
