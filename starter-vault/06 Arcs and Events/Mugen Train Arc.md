# Mugen Train Arc

> [!events] At a glance
> The arc before [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]]'s, starring [[Kyojuro Rengoku]].

## Overview

The Mugen Train Arc comes immediately before the [[Entertainment District Arc]] and centres on [[Kyojuro Rengoku]], the [[Kyojuro Rengoku|Flame Hashira]], with [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] and [[Inosuke Hashibira]]. It sets up the next mission handed down by the [[Demon Slayer Corps]], led by [[Tengen Uzui]].
