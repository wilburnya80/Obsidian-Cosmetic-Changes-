# Blood Demon Art

> [!concepts] At a glance
> The supernatural abilities unique to demons.

## Overview

Blood Demon Art is the term for the supernatural abilities each strong demon possesses. Examples here are [[Daki's Obi]] and [[Gyutaro]]'s [[Flying Blood Sickles]], both of which belong to the [[Twelve Kizuki]].

- **Examples:** [[Daki's Obi]], [[Flying Blood Sickles]]
- **Users:** [[Daki]], [[Gyutaro]]
- **Group:** [[Twelve Kizuki]]
