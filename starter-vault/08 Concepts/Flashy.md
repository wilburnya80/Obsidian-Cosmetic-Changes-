# Flashy

> [!concepts] At a glance
> [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]]'s creed: be bold, loud and unforgettable.

## Overview

Flashy is the creed of [[Tengen Uzui]], and he calls himself the [[Tengen Uzui|Flashy God]]. It explains his [[Explosive Grenades]], his [[Jeweled Headband]] and the [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]]. It is also the design principle of this theme: the [[Rooftop Fireworks Stand]] has to be loud.

## Key facts

- **Practitioner:** [[Tengen Uzui]]
- **Shown by:** [[Explosive Grenades]], [[Jeweled Headband]], [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]]
- **Theme principle:** [[Rooftop Fireworks Stand]]
