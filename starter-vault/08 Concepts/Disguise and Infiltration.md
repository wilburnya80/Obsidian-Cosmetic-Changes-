# Disguise and Infiltration

> [!concepts] At a glance
> Going undercover: the core method of the [[Entertainment District Arc]].

## Overview

Disguise and Infiltration is the tactic that drives the [[Entertainment District Arc]]. [[Tengen Uzui]] uses his shinobi background and his wives [[Makio]], [[Suma]] and [[Hinatsuru]] to plant slayers inside the [[Entertainment District]]'s okiya, including [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] and [[Inosuke Hashibira]]. See the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]].
