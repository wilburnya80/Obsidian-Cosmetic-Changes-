# Poison

> [!concepts] At a glance
> [[Gyutaro]]'s deadly blood poison.

## Overview

Poison is the lethal substance in [[Gyutaro]]'s blood and on [[Gyutaro's Sickles]]. It supercharges the [[Flying Blood Sickles]] and is the main reason [[Tengen Uzui]] is in danger during the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]].
