# Breathing Styles

> [!concepts] At a glance
> The sword techniques of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]], each based on a way of breathing.

## Overview

Breathing Styles are the core fighting techniques of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]]. This vault tracks [[Sound Breathing]], [[Thunder Breathing]], [[Beast Breathing]], [[Water Breathing]] and [[Hinokami Kagura]], each used with a [[Nichirin Blade]].
