# Upper Rank Six

> [!concepts] At a glance
> The sixth Upper seat of the [[Twelve Kizuki]], shared by [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]].

## Overview

Upper Rank Six is the sixth seat among the [[Twelve Kizuki|Upper Moons]] of the [[Twelve Kizuki]], held jointly by [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]]. Defeating both is the goal of the [[Entertainment District Arc]] and the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]].

- **Holders:** [[Daki]], [[Gyutaro]]
- **Group:** [[Twelve Kizuki]]
- **Defeated in:** [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]]
