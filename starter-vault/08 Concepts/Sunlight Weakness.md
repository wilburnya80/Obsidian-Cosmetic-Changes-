# Sunlight Weakness

> [!concepts] At a glance
> Demons cannot survive direct sunlight.

## Overview

Sunlight Weakness is the rule that demons, from [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]] up to [[Muzan Kibitsuji]], cannot survive direct sunlight. It is why the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] hunts at night and why the [[Nichirin Blade]] is forged from sun-charged steel.

## Key facts

- **Applies to:** all demons
- **Exploited by:** [[Nichirin Blade]]
- **Counters:** [[Twelve Kizuki]]
