# Demon Slayer Mark

> [!concepts] At a glance
> A mark that appears on a slayer who pushes past their limits.

## Overview

A Demon Slayer Mark is the mark that appears on a slayer who pushes far beyond their usual limits. [[Tanjiro Kamado]] awakens one against [[Gyutaro]] in the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]], a turning point of the [[Entertainment District Arc]].
