# Manga

> [!sources] At a glance
> The original manga, which this vault's black-and-white light mode imitates.

## Overview

The Manga is the original source for the [[Entertainment District Arc]], [[Tengen Uzui]] and the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]]. The black-ink look of its panels is the inspiration for the light mode of this theme, "Manga Panel".

## Key facts

- **Covers:** [[Entertainment District Arc]], [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]]
- **Inspires:** the Manga Panel light mode
- **Adapted as:** [[Anime Season 2]]
