# Manga

> [!sources] At a glance
> The original manga, whose bold black outlines inspire this vault's pixel look.

## Overview

The Manga is the original source for the [[Entertainment District Arc]], [[Tengen Uzui]] and the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]]. The bold black outlines of its panels inspire the pixel-art look of this theme.

## Key facts

- **Covers:** [[Entertainment District Arc]], [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]]
- **Inspires:** the theme's bold pixel outlines
- **Adapted as:** [[Anime Season 2]]
