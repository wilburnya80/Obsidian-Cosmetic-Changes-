# Gyutaro's Sickles

> [!weapons] At a glance
> The sickles [[Gyutaro]] throws and wields, coated in his own poison.

## Overview

Gyutaro's Sickles are the paired blades of [[Gyutaro]], used with the [[Flying Blood Sickles]] technique and coated in his [[Poison]]. They are a counterpart to the [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]] of [[Tengen Uzui]] in the fights of the [[Entertainment District Arc]].
