# Twin Nichirin Cleavers

> [!weapons] At a glance
> [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]]'s two enormous cleaver-like [[Nichirin Blade|Nichirin]] swords, joined by a chain.

## Overview

The Twin Nichirin Cleavers are [[Tengen Uzui]]'s weapons: two oversized [[Nichirin Blade]]-forged cleavers joined by a chain, always drawn together. They are the heart of [[Sound Breathing]] and the image at the centre of the [[Rooftop Fireworks Stand]]. They are carried with his [[Explosive Grenades]] and the [[Jeweled Headband]] completes the look.

## Key facts

- **Owner:** [[Tengen Uzui]]
- **Pair:** two blades joined by a chain
- **Forms:** [[Constant Resounding Slashes]], [[String Performance]]
