# Explosive Grenades

> [!weapons] At a glance
> [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]]'s gunpowder grenades, the loud half of his flashy fighting style.

## Overview

Explosive Grenades are the gunpowder bombs [[Tengen Uzui]] uses alongside the [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]]. They fit the [[Flashy]] creed: bright, loud and impossible to ignore. They are used in the fight against [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]] and echo the fireworks of the [[Rooftop Fireworks Stand]].
