# Nichirin Blade

> [!weapons] At a glance
> The sun-forged sword every demon slayer carries.

## Overview

A Nichirin Blade is the sword that every member of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] carries, forged from steel that can kill demons. [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] and [[Inosuke Hashibira]] all use one, and [[Tengen Uzui]]'s version is the extreme case: the [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]].

## Key facts

- **Carried by:** [[Demon Slayer Corps]]
- **Extreme variant:** [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]]
- **Kills:** demons, via the [[Sunlight Weakness]] link to the steel
