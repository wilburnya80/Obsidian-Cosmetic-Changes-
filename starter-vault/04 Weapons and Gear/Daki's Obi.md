# Daki's Obi

> [!weapons] At a glance
> The living obi sashes [[Daki]] uses as blades, snares and shields.

## Overview

Daki's Obi are the sashes [[Daki]] commands as weapons, snares and storage for the people she has taken. They are an expression of her [[Blood Demon Art]] and a major hazard for [[Tengen Uzui]], [[Tanjiro Kamado]] and [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] in the [[Entertainment District]].
