# Giyu Tomioka

> [!characters] At a glance
> Water Hashira and [[Demon Slayer Corps|the Corps]] member who first pointed [[Tanjiro Kamado|Tanjiro]] toward the slayers.

## Overview

Giyu Tomioka is the Water Hashira and a user of [[Water Breathing]]. As a fellow [[Hashira]] he sits alongside [[Tengen Uzui]], [[Shinobu Kocho]] and [[Kyojuro Rengoku]] in the [[Demon Slayer Corps]], and is closely tied to [[Tanjiro Kamado]]'s origins.
