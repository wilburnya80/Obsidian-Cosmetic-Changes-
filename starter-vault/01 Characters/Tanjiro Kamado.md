# Tanjiro Kamado

> [!characters] At a glance
> Kind-hearted demon slayer who joins [[Tengen Uzui|Tengen]]'s mission and awakens a [[Demon Slayer Mark]] against [[Gyutaro]].

## Overview

Tanjiro Kamado is the central demon slayer of the story, wielding a [[Nichirin Blade]] and the [[Hinokami Kagura]]. In the [[Entertainment District Arc]] he goes undercover in the [[Entertainment District]] as part of [[Tengen Uzui]]'s plan, learning [[Disguise and Infiltration]] the hard way, and later fights [[Gyutaro]] alongside [[Inosuke Hashibira]] and [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]]. During that fight he awakens a [[Demon Slayer Mark]].

He is also the link to [[Giyu Tomioka]] and the [[Water Breathing]] he first learned.
