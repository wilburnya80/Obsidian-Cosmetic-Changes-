# Inosuke Hashibira

> [!characters] At a glance
> Wild [[Beast Breathing]] slayer who fights [[Gyutaro]] beside [[Tanjiro Kamado|Tanjiro]].

## Overview

Inosuke Hashibira fights with two jagged [[Nichirin Blade|Nichirin Blades]] and [[Beast Breathing]], a style he invented himself. He is sent into the [[Entertainment District]] with [[Tanjiro Kamado]] and [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] under [[Tengen Uzui]]'s orders and plays a decisive part in the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]].
