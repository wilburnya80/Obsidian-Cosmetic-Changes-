# Kyojuro Rengoku

> [!characters] At a glance

## Overview

Kyojuro Rengoku is the Flame Hashira and the central figure of the [[Mugen Train Arc]], which comes directly before the [[Entertainment District Arc]]. He is a fellow [[Hashira]] with [[Tengen Uzui]] and [[Giyu Tomioka]] and a reminder of what is at stake for the [[Demon Slayer Corps]].
