# Makio

> [!characters] At a glance
> One of [[Tengen Uzui]]'s three wives, a kunoichi who infiltrated the [[Entertainment District]] before the others.

## Overview

Makio is one of [[Tengen Uzui]]'s three wives and a trained kunoichi from the [[Shinobi Clan]] background they share. She belongs to the [[Uzui Household]] and was sent into the [[Entertainment District]] ahead of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] to gather information for the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]]. She is the sharpest-tongued of the three.
