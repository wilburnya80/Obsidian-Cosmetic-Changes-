# Tengen Uzui

> [!characters] At a glance
> Retired shinobi and Sound Hashira who calls himself the Flashy God and fights with two chained cleavers.

## Overview

Tengen Uzui is the Sound Hashira of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]]. Born into a [[Shinobi Clan]] and trained as a shinobi, he left that life behind and now lives as the husband of [[Makio]], [[Suma]] and [[Hinatsuru]], who together form the [[Uzui Household]]. He wears a [[Jeweled Headband]] and fights with the [[Twin Nichirin Cleavers]], two huge blades joined by a chain, using [[Sound Breathing]], his [[Score]] and a pouch of [[Explosive Grenades]].

In the [[Entertainment District Arc]] he recruits [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] and [[Inosuke Hashibira]] for the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]], then takes on [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]] in the [[Final Battle of Daki and Gyutaro]]. His stand on the [[Yoshiwara Rooftops]] is the visual this whole theme is built around: see [[Rooftop Fireworks Stand]]. After the arc he steps down as a [[Hashira]] ([[Tengen Retires]]).
