# Kagaya Ubuyashiki

> [!characters] At a glance
> Head of the [[Ubuyashiki Clan]] and leader of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]].

## Overview

Kagaya Ubuyashiki leads the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] from the [[Ubuyashiki Mansion]] and is the head of the [[Ubuyashiki Clan]]. He commands the [[Hashira]], including [[Tengen Uzui]], [[Shinobu Kocho]] and [[Giyu Tomioka]], and is the ultimate authority behind missions like the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]].
