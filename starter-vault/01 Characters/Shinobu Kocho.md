# Shinobu Kocho

> [!characters] At a glance
> Insect Hashira who runs the [[Butterfly Mansion]] and its recovery wards.

## Overview

Shinobu Kocho is the Insect Hashira. She heads the [[Butterfly Mansion]], where wounded slayers such as [[Tanjiro Kamado]] recover, and serves under [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki]] with the other [[Hashira]] including [[Giyu Tomioka]] and [[Tengen Uzui]].
