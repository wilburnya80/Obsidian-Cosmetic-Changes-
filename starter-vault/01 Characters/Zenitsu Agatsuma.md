# Zenitsu Agatsuma

> [!characters] At a glance
> [[Thunder Breathing]] slayer who goes undercover in the [[Entertainment District|Yoshiwara]] and fights the [[Upper Rank Six]] siblings.

## Overview

Zenitsu Agatsuma uses [[Thunder Breathing]] and is the fastest of the trio. For the [[Infiltration of Yoshiwara]] he is placed in an okiya in the [[Entertainment District]], then joins [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Inosuke Hashibira]] and [[Tengen Uzui]] in the battle against [[Daki]] and [[Gyutaro]]. His [[Nichirin Blade]] is his one constant.
